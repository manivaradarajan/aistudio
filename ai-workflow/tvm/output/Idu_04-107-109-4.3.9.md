**Verse 9**

உரைக்கவல்லேனல்லேன் உன்னுலபில்கீர்த்திவெள்ளத்தின்
கரைக்கணென்றுசெல்வன்நான் காதல்மையலேறினேன்
புரைப்பிலாதபரம்பரனே பொய்யிலாதபரஞ்சுடரே
இரைத்துநல்லமேன்மக்களேத்த யானுமேத்தினேன்.

**Verse Translation**
I have not the power to speak of You.
When shall I ever reach the shore
of Your limitless flood of glory?
I am overcome by the delirium of love.
O flawless Supreme Lord! O true Supreme Splendor!
As the noble great ones roar and praise, I too have praised.

**ஆ – (6000 Commentary)**
I am not able to speak of You. If asked, "Could you not speak of the parts that are possible?" I would reply: Can one ever reach even the shores of the limitless flood of glory that is Your union with me? If this glory is so inaccessible, why do You speak of it? Because, O Lord, You showed Yourself to me as You truly are, and then, with no other motive, You united with me, bearing that very union as Your limitless splendor! Seeing how the ever-vigilant celestials, filled with supreme devotion, became enraptured by this union and praised You, I too, with a similar excess of devotion, became enraptured and praised You.

**ஒ – (9000 Commentary)**
In the ninth verse, the Alvar declares, "The supreme excellence of Your loving nature is beyond the scope of my speech."

"When shall I ever reach the shore of the flood of Your limitless glory?" Even if I were to speak only of what is possible, I could never reach even the shore of Your immeasurable quality of love. "I am overcome by the delirium of love." "If so, why did You begin this endeavor?" I lost my senses because of my great affection. "O flawless Supreme Being! O true Supreme Splendor!" O You who, revealing Your quality of love for me without any defect, possess the very union with me as Your limitless splendor! This may also mean that the excellence of Your love and the resulting excellence of Your joy are visible in Your divine form. "As the noble great ones roar and praise, I too have praised." Just as the ever-vigilant celestials, filled with supreme devotion, praised You, exerting themselves according to their capacity, I too praised You.

**ப – (12000 Commentary)**
Next, the Alvar asks, "How can I ever finish speaking of You, who possess true supremacy and splendor?"

*I am not able to speak:* I do not have the capacity to capture in verse Your quality of love for Your dependents. (Why is that?) *When shall I reach the shore of the flood of Your limitless fame?* When will I ever reach the shore of the ocean that is Your fame, which flows from Your loving nature? (If so, why did I begin to speak?) *I am overcome by the delirium of love:* I became greatly agitated on account of my love. *O flawless Supreme Being:* O You who possess true and blemishless supremacy over all! *O true Supreme Splendor:* O You who, because Your union with me is without falsehood, possess a splendor that is as matchless as Your supremacy! *As the noble great ones roar and praise:* As the eternal beings—exalted above all, possessing a sublime nature of knowledge and other qualities—roar like a rising ocean and praise Him, *I too have praised:* I, overcome by love, simply praised as well; that is all.

**இ – (24000 Commentary)**
In the ninth verse, when asked, "What is the difficulty in speaking of what you have experienced?" the Alvar replies, "The supreme excellence of Your loving nature is beyond the scope of my speech."

"I am not able to speak," and so on. Thinking that this was a suitable subject for me, I tried, but how can I possibly put Your quality of love into verse? If I tried to speak of what is within my power, when would I ever reach the shore of the flood of fame that arose from Your limitless love? It is impossible, at any time. "If so, why did you begin to speak?" I became deluded, blinded by love. "What was the basis for this delusion?" I spoke because I saw the eternal beings praising Him. In this matter, there is truly no difference between the eternal beings and this Alvar.

"O flawless Supreme Being! O true Supreme Splendor!" O You who appear with the splendor from Your union with me shining on Your form, a splendor that is as flawless as the existence of Your boundless quality of love! Alternatively: Just as there is no flaw in Your status as the "Supreme over all," You are equally without flaw in the splendor that arose from Your union with me.

"As the noble great ones roar," and so on. I praised You only because I saw the eternal beings, filled with supreme devotion, roaring like a rising ocean as they praised You. Did I speak because I was capable of it?

**ஈடு: – (36000 Commentary)**
Ninth verse. When asked, “What is the difficulty in speaking of what you have experienced?” the Alvar replies, “The supreme excellence of Your loving nature is beyond the scope of my speech.”

“I am not able to speak.” I can only experience Your loving nature and be overwhelmed into silence; I cannot speak of it and bring it to completion. If you cannot speak of it all, can you not at least speak of the parts that are describable? He replies: “Of the flood of Your limitless fame…” Can I even reach the shore of the ocean of fame that arose from Your boundless loving nature? If it is a subject so difficult that one cannot even approach its shore, why did you endeavor to speak of it? He answers: “I am overcome by the delirium of love.” I became utterly distraught due to my love. Can one question a madman, asking, “Why are you doing this?” But why are *you* in such a state over this subject? Does one who is entranced not need some basis for it? He replies, “I saw the eternal beings, themselves maddened with love, praising Him, and that is why I did it.”

Indeed, in this matter, there is no difference between the eternal beings, the Alvar, and even the Lord Himself. Even if He, the omniscient and omnipotent One, were to try to know Himself, He would find His own nature difficult to comprehend. He too would suffer the same fate as the Vedas that tried to know Him and had to turn back, declaring, “From where words turn back” (Taittiriya Upanishad 2.4.1 [?]).

“O flawless Supreme Being! O true Supreme Splendor!” This means: O You, who are as flawless in the splendor that now adorns Your form from uniting with me as You are in Your state of being supreme over all! Alternatively, it means: O You who reveal in Your form the splendor that comes from Your union with me—a union without falsehood—just as there is no flaw in the very existence of Your boundless quality of love!

“As the noble great ones roar and praise, I too have praised.” He concludes: I praised Him only because I saw the eternal beings, filled with supreme devotion, roaring like a rising ocean as they praised Him. Did I praise Him because I myself was capable of it?
