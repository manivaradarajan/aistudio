**Verse Translation**

The anointing paste is my heart itself;
the cherished garland, one woven from my words.
The fine silk garment is that very same thing,
and the radiant jewels are my joined palms in prayer—
all for the singular Lord, my Father, the Sovereign who consumed and spit out the world.

**ஆ – (6000 Commentary)**

For the Lord, who resides in Sri Vaikuntha with a singular form and whose essential nature is that of the Supreme Lord who swallowed and spit out all the worlds, each and every one of my actions directed toward Him—such as remembrance, praise, and the joining of my palms—has become all the articles of enjoyment: the divine fragrant paste, the divine garlands and garments, and the divine ornaments of unsurpassed radiance. The Alvar exclaims, “Behold the extent of one's deep love!”

**ஒ - (9000 Commentary)**

In the second verse, he delights, saying, “My acts of remembrance and the like have become for Him the fragrant paste for His limbs and other adornments!”

[**Anointing paste**] A paste that cannot be worn unless it is applied. [**Cherished garland**] A floral garland that is worn with great desire. [**Fine silk garment**] An exceedingly soft sacred garment. [**Radiant jewels**] Lustrous sacred jewels. [**For the Sovereign who consumed…**] For the one who is the Supreme Lord, the protector of the world in numerous ways, who through that very act has especially become my Master, and who possesses a unique and beautiful sacred form.

**ப – (12000 Commentary)**

Next, the Alvar says, “My three faculties are becoming objects of enjoyment for the Master, who is the controller of all and the protector of all.”

**Sovereign**: The unconditional controller.
**Who consumed and spit out the world**: As one who consumes and spits out during the distress of the cosmic dissolution, He is the protector of all things in every way.
**My Father**: My Master, who enslaved me through this glorious deed.
**Singular**: Peerless, because He is an object of unsurpassed enjoyment.
**For the form**: For the one who possesses the sacred form.
**Anointing paste**: The fitting paste applied to Him, like the one offered by the hunchbacked woman, is my heart itself.
**Cherished garland**: The sacred garland that He lovingly wears, like the one from the garland-maker, is the garland woven from the words of my speech.
**Fine silk garment**: The excellent silk garment is also that very same activity of speech, just as Tiruanantālvan offered a garment described as “the flowered silk.”
**Radiant jewels**: The ornaments worn to bestow radiance are the very act of my joined palms.

*   “Words becoming a silk garment” means they add to the splendor and completeness of His adornment.
*   “The joined palms becoming an ornament” means that the reverential gesture the Alvar places on his own head brings a brilliant radiance to the Lord, just like the ornaments worn on His sacred crown after the consecrating bath.

**இ - (24000 Commentary)**

In the second verse, the Alvar exclaims, “The actions of my faculties, such as remembrance, born of my own deep yearning, have themselves become all the articles of enjoyment for Him, the All-Perfect One!”

Seeing how He desired even an insentient faculty as an object of enjoyment, the Alvar is so overwhelmed with love that he repeats himself, saying again, “The anointing paste is my very heart.” His mental attachment has become a fragrant paste for the Lord’s limbs. Not a common garland, but one He cherishes and wears—this is merely my utterance. His speech itself becomes fragrant to his own heart, for it is a garland woven of words. After all, it is the tied flowers that give fragrance to the sandalwood paste.

[**The fine silk garment is that very same thing**] It is not that one thing becomes only one article of enjoyment; even the sacred garment that asserts His supreme masculinity is nothing but my utterance! [**Fine silk garment**] A sacred garment of great breadth and value. [**The radiant jewels...**] The radiance-bestowing sacred ornament is my own reverential gesture! Just like the famed *Rājamahendran-paṭi* ornament, the Alvar's joined palms have become a source of excellence for Him.

If one asks, “Is this reverential gesture bestowing radiance upon one who is somehow deficient?”—No! It is for the [**Sovereign**], the Supreme Lord. If one wonders, “Would one who is merely called ‘Protector’ allow his possession to suffer?”—No! He is the one [**who consumed and spit out the world**], the protector of the world in every way. [**My Father**] He is the one who claimed me through that very act of protection. [**For the singular form**] For the One who possesses a peerless sacred form. The Alvar exclaims, “For the One who possesses the sacred form known as ‘All-Powerful,’ the anointing paste has become my heart!”

**ஈடு: – (36000 Commentary)**

In this second verse, the Alvar says, “The very actions of my faculties, such as remembrance, have become all the articles of enjoyment for Him, the All-Perfect One.”

[**The anointing paste is my heart itself**] Meditating on the excellence of this sacred form and on the Lord's profound desire for him, the Alvar is overwhelmed and says it again.

[**The cherished garland**] This is not an ordinary, everyday garland, but one that is offered with special reverence.

[**The garland woven from my words**] The Alvar need not even say, “This is for Him.” If it belongs to the Alvar, it is automatically His.

[**The garland woven from my words**] It seems his speech surpasses even his heart in fragrance. After all, it is the strung flowers that impart their scent to the sandalwood paste.

[**The fine silk garment is that very same thing**] That very utterance is also the sacred garment that signifies His supreme masculinity. The Alvar’s sublime state of mind is the fine thread, and because it is such a fine thread, it has become a fine silk garment. He is one who, from the very beginning, performed penances in order to weave this fine scripture.

[**The radiant jewels**] The jewels that bestow radiance upon Himself.

[**Are the very act of my joined palms**] Like the famous *Cērapāṇṭiyan Tampirāṉ* pendant, the Alvar's reverential gesture becomes a source of radiance for Him.

Is the one who gains such splendor from these things someone who is in any way deficient? [**Sovereign**] He is the Supreme Lord of all. If one wonders, “Would one who is merely called ‘Protector’ allow his possession to suffer?” No! He is the one [**who consumed and spit out the world**], the protector of the world in every way. [**My Father**] He is the one who claimed me through that very act of protection. [**For the singular form**] For the One who possesses a peerless sacred form.
